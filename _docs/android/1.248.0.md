## No more stacked error messages when the phone switches from Wi-Fi to mobile data

If you opened a gate or lock at the edge of your Wi-Fi range, the phone's switch to mobile data could show the same error several times in a row. The app now waits for the new connection to answer, shows at most one message, and the lock and gate screens keep working without a manual refresh.

## Auto-Unlock works with the phone screen off

On some phones Auto-Unlock only worked once the screen was turned on, because the phone paused the Bluetooth search in the background. The app now also connects to the lock directly as you approach, so the door unlocks with the phone still in your pocket.
