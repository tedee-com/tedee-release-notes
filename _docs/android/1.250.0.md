## Copy the configuration of an existing lock to a new one

When you replace a lock or add another one, you can now reuse the setup of a device you already manage. At the end of the new lock's setup, choose the source device and what to bring over: settings, user access, PIN codes and fingerprints, and paired accessories such as the keypad, fingerprint reader, door sensor or dry contact. Accessories are unpaired from the source device and paired with the new lock, and PIN codes move together with the keypad.

## American English

The app now uses American English spelling and wording when your phone is set to English (United States). Other English variants keep the current texts.
