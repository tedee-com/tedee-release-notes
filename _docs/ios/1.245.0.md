## Change account password in the app

Tedee account holders can now change their password directly in the app, from the Profile page. Simply go to your Profile and use the new password change option to update your credentials.

Please note that this feature is available only for users signed in with a Tedee account (email and password). If you use Sign in with Apple, Google, or Microsoft, password management is handled by your respective identity provider and is not available in the Tedee app.

## Finnish language

Added Finnish language support. To switch to Finnish, go to your iPhone system settings and set Finnish as your preferred language for the Tedee app under **iOS Settings → Apps → Tedee → Language**.
