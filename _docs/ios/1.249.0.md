## You can now control locks and gates by voice without creating shortcuts, and it works with CarPlay too

Siri integration has been rebuilt to work with the latest Apple technology. You can now control your devices by voice using their names, with these phrases:

- "Open *lock name* with tedee"
- "Close *lock name* with tedee"
- "Open *gate name* with tedee"

The same actions are also available in the Shortcuts app.

## Improved auto-unlock reliability

The app now reconnects to your lock more reliably during auto-unlock, so your door unlocks as you arrive.

## Fixed an issue where Bluetooth unlock could fail for guest users without remote access

Guest users who don't have remote access can now unlock via Bluetooth reliably.

## Fixed an issue with adding Keypad PRO

Resolved problems that could occur when adding a Keypad PRO to your account.
